HOW TO PLAY

Survive the zombie waves. After each wave, pick 1 of 3 power-ups: Rare · Epic · Legendary. Loot guns under the light beams; glowing mutated guns have special powers. Climb cars, walls, wrecks and ladders. Build barricades and turrets with scrap from kills.

Keyboard & mouse

WASD move
Mouse aim · Click shoot
Right click zoom / scope
R reload
Shift sprint
C crouch · slide while sprinting
Space jump / climb
1-8 / wheel switch gun · Q last
V knife stab · 2 knife
G throw grenade
B build barricades & turrets
E revive a teammate (co-op)
Z emotes
Tab scores · T chat (online)

Controller

Left stick move · right stick look · RT fire · LT aim · A jump · B crouch · X reload (hold to revive) · Y swap · LB grenade · RB knife · R3 emotes · D-pad up build · Start pause

Touch

Left thumb moves (a joystick appears), drag on the right to look, buttons on the right for everything else.
